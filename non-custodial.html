Non-custodial: You always hold your keys

GalaxySwap never holds your assets, never retains your private keys, and never requires centralized accounts. Every transaction executes straight from your self-custody wallet directly to verified on-chain smart contracts.

How GalaxySwap Protects Your Sovereignty

Centralized exchanges and custodial bridges hold user funds in pooled hot wallets, exposing users to counterparty insolvency, withdrawal freezes, and systemic hacks. GalaxySwap is completely client-side:

🔐 Client-Side Signatures

Every token swap, liquidity deposit, or bridge execution is packaged into raw calldata in your browser and signed by your local wallet (MetaMask, Rainbow, Coinbase Wallet, etc.).

⚡ Atomic Settlement

Transactions route through audited DEX protocols (Uniswap V3) and cross-chain bridges (LI.FI). If any leg of a transaction fails, it reverts immediately—funds never get stuck in limbo.

🚫 No Custodial Middlemen

GalaxySwap operates zero centralized deposit contracts, zero custodial holding wallets, and zero withdrawal queues. Your keys, your crypto.

🛡️ Permissionless & Censorship Resistant

Even if GalaxySwap's web servers were offline, your positions are permanently on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, and Polygon and redeemable through any standard RPC node.
